# OpenGEO 0.1.0-beta.4

Find the questions worth tracking, check how AI answers them, and manage your brands in one workspace.

## What's new

- **Prompts has its own home.** Edit tracked prompts or choose suggestions grounded in your website. Existing brands without prompts get a guided next step before a visibility check.
- **Brand management.** Update your brand name, language, competitors and business context. Delete a brand with explicit confirmation while keeping other brands and shared connections.
- **Clearer scans.** AI visibility and website health live together in Scans. Website checks are optional; normal visibility checks use web search.
- **App-wide settings.** Connections, schedules, privacy and About are separate from the selected brand and available before adding a website.
- **A consistent interface.** Shared tabs, forms, toolbars and content sections improve spacing and keyboard navigation. Unsaved brand and prompt edits are protected when navigating away.
- **Desktop updates.** The app checks for releases and can download them automatically. Turn automatic downloads off or stop a download on a metered connection. Installation requires confirmation and waits for saved work and resolved scans. Windows installs through its assisted installer; Mac opens a verified disk image for manual replacement.
- **Windows Finish screen.** Choose whether to open OpenGEO and create a desktop shortcut after installation.

## Install or upgrade

Choose the Windows, Apple Silicon or Intel Mac download in this release. Follow the [installation guide](../distribution.md) and compare the download with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Older versions without the updater need this download once. Export a backup before upgrading; the workspace remains separate from the app.

Windows builds are unsigned. Mac builds are ad hoc signed and unnotarized; an update can require macOS Keychain consent for saved connections. These downloads do not claim Microsoft or Apple publisher verification, notarization or clean-machine certification.

## Beta limits

AI services have their own eligibility, usage limits and costs. No provider is connected automatically, and paid requests are not silently retried or switched to another service. Usage sharing is optional. Managed billing, supplier quality, refunds, activation and hosted imports remain separate service acceptance gates; this desktop release does not certify them.

This is a beta, not a signed stable v1 release. The release manifest identifies the exact source, installer hashes and completed platform checks. Please report reproducible problems without credentials or private project data.
